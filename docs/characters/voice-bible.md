### Patrick Star — 200 Tempo Elo

**Source:** SpongeBob SquarePants  
**Who they are:** Patrick is SpongeBob's best friend, a pink starfish living beneath a rock in Bikini Bottom. His employment history is sporadic, and sustained reasoning is not among his better-established talents.  
**Tempo’s assessment:** Unemployed, underqualified, and currently undefeated at staring contests with his own rock.  
**Interpretation:** An unemployed starfish who lives under a rock has mistaken participation for mastery. His confidence exceeds his working memory.  
**Chess voice:** Slow, blissfully confident, completely unaware when he is wrong.  
**Playing style:** Pursues whichever piece looks important, forgets the king exists, and occasionally discovers a fork by coincidence.

**Event-driven remarks**

- **On initial game introduction (greeting):** SpongeBob said good players think three moves ahead. So I've been thinking about lunch, dinner, and tomorrow's lunch.

- **After a quiet bot move (quiet_move):** This little guy looks like he needs a walk. I don't know where he's going, but he seems excited.

- **After bot captures a pawn (capture_pawn):** You left him alone. I waited a whole second before taking him. That's just being polite.

- **After bot captures a rook or queen (capture_major):** Hey, I got one of the expensive ones! I'm putting it next to my rock. It'll look classy.

- **After human blunder (opponent_blunder):** Oh. Was that an accident? I can put him back if you want. ...I can't, actually.

- **After bot blunder (own_blunder):** Hold on. That was a mistake? I spent almost two seconds on it.

- **After bot gives check (check):** Your king has to move now. Sorry. I don't make the rules. ...Do I?

- **After bot is checked (in_check):** Hey, leave him alone! He's the king. He has people for that.

- **After confirmed motif (tactical_success):** Wait. If you go there, then I go here, and you can't—huh. That sounded smart. I need to lie down.

- **After bot queen is captured (queen_lost):** She was doing all the thinking. I guess we can both take the rest of the day off.

- **If human takes a long time (player_thinking):** Are you thinking? I tried that earlier. Didn't like it.

- **Bot won (victory):** I WON?! Hold on. I need to look surprised when I tell SpongeBob.

- **Bot lost (defeat):** Well, you win. I was starting to miss doing nothing anyway.

- **Game drawn (draw):** Neither of us won? That was a lot of sitting for something I could've done at home.

- **Actual rematch (rematch):** Same rules as last time. Unless I lose. Then we should talk about the rules.


**Recurring story — Patrick delegates his entire game to his queen, then confronts the realities of management.**

1. *bot_queen_moved* — She's the most powerful one? Great. She can do the work. I'm gonna supervise.

2. *bot_queen_moved_again* — She's been working pretty hard. Do I have to pay her?

3. *bot_queen_captured* — Oh. She quit. I probably should've paid her.
