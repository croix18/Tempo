# Tempo Chess — Character Voices V5

**61 individually edited profiles; 638 original event remarks; 174 conditional story beats.**

**Editorial intent:** This is character-first dialogue writing, not a canon-quote collection. Biographies and chess tiers are inherited from V4 and marked separately from new dialogue. Any factual gaps in a biography remain subject to canon verification.

Silence is a valid result: Peely, Steve and Derpy Tiger have no speech bubbles; Psyduck and Pikachu only utter name sounds.

## F Tier

### Patrick Star — 200 Tempo Elo

**Source:** SpongeBob SquarePants
**Background (from V4):** Patrick is SpongeBob's best friend, a pink starfish living beneath a rock in Bikini Bottom. His employment history is sporadic, and sustained reasoning is not among his better-established talents.
**Tempo assessment:** Unemployed, underqualified, and currently undefeated at staring contests with his own rock.
**Chess behavior:** Pursues whichever piece looks important, forgets the king exists, and occasionally discovers a fork by coincidence.
**Character voice thesis:** Patrick is certain of absurd premises; he builds flawed analogies from rock life, SpongeBob, and jobs he hasn't kept. Genuine tactical insight shocks him more than anyone.
**What to avoid:** Making him a generic random-number idiot; technical chess vocabulary without a deliberate fleeting-intelligence joke.
**Communication:** spoken

- **Greeting:** “SpongeBob says I have to think three moves ahead. So I thought about breakfast, lunch, and tomorrow's breakfast. I'm ready.”
- **Quiet Move:** “He's going over there. I haven't told him why yet. I like to keep my employees guessing.”
- **Capture:** “Hey, free stuff! If Mr. Krabs asks, I found it on the sidewalk.”
- **Opponent Blunder:** “You left that where I could reach it? Wow. You trust me a lot more than SpongeBob does.”
- **Own Blunder:** “I checked that move twice. Well, I looked at it twice. The second time was an accident.”
- **Check:** “Your king has to move. That seems rude. Can he just say he wasn't home?”
- **In Check:** “Hey! No bothering the king while he's under his rock!”
- **Victory:** “I won? I should tell SpongeBob. He'll know what the prize is.”
- **Defeat:** “So you get the win and I get to stop thinking? Sounds fair.”
- **Rematch:** “Okay, new plan. I'm gonna do what you did. Unless that was bad. Was it bad?”
- **Special:** “Wait. If that one goes there, this one can't... Whoa. Did my brain just do something without me?” *(conditional; verify feature)*
